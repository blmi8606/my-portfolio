01 / Project Overview

Since May 2020, I have independently explored hydroponic cultivation through the design and maintenance of at-home growing systems for plants and microgreens.

This work involves studying how water quality, nutrient availability, and environmental conditions influence plant growth in soil-free systems.

02 / Engineering Challenge

Hydroponic systems require careful management of the growing environment. Changes in nutrient concentration, water quality, and environmental conditions can influence plant health and system performance.

My focus is understanding these relationships and exploring practical adjustments that support reliable cultivation.

03 / Research & Development Methods

01 — System Design

Design and maintain at-home hydroponic growing systems, considering plant support, water availability, and system maintenance.

02 — Nutrient Management

Monitor and adjust nutrient conditions to support plant development and explore the relationship between nutrient availability and growth.

03 — Water Quality

Observe water conditions and investigate their importance in maintaining a healthy hydroponic growing environment.

04 — Environmental Monitoring

Monitor cultivation conditions and plant growth progression to identify opportunities for system improvements.

04 / Engineering Applications

Hydroponics provides opportunities to apply principles of fluid systems, mass transport, biological processes, environmental control, and process optimization.

This project strengthens my understanding of how engineering principles can be applied to sustainable biological production.

05 / Challenges & Problem-Solving

Maintaining a hydroponic system involves balancing multiple environmental and nutritional variables.

Through ongoing experimentation, I evaluate system conditions, troubleshoot cultivation challenges, and make adjustments intended to support healthy plant growth.

06 / Skills & Learning Outcomes

This independent project has provided hands-on experience in system maintenance, environmental monitoring, experimental observation, and engineering problem-solving.

07 / Future Development

Future opportunities include incorporating automated monitoring, investigating sensor-based environmental control, and collecting quantitative data to evaluate system performance.

These are potential improvements rather than completed system features.
