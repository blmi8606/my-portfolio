Project Overview

As a research intern in the NEU Lab, I contributed to the investigation of cartilage-mimicking biomaterials using confocal microscopy and computational image analysis.

Research Objectives

My work focused on analyzing microscopy images and exploring methods to improve the consistency and reproducibility of quantitative image analysis.

Technical Methods

  • Confocal imaging of cartilage-mimicking biomaterials.
  • MATLAB segmentation using manual, adaptive, global, and Otsu thresholding techniques.
  • Area-fraction analysis to quantify selected image regions.
  • Cross-validation of MATLAB results using ImageJ.

My Contributions

I developed and compared image segmentation workflows in MATLAB, evaluated their suitability for microscopy analysis, and used ImageJ to cross-check analysis outputs.
