01 / Project Overview

My independent mycology research focuses on exploring mushroom cultivation and understanding how environmental conditions influence fungal growth.

Through hands-on experimentation, I investigate cultivation methods, prepare growing materials, maintain controlled conditions, and document growth progression.

02 / Research Objectives

The primary objective is to develop a practical understanding of fungal cultivation and the environmental factors that influence growth outcomes.

  • Monitor environmental conditions throughout cultivation.
  • Explore substrate preparation and cultivation methods.
  • Document biological growth and experimental observations.
  • Identify and troubleshoot contamination or growth challenges.

03 / Experimental Methods

01 — Environmental Monitoring

Observe temperature, humidity, substrate conditions, and growth progression to understand their relationship with cultivation outcomes.

02 — Cultivation Preparation

Research cultivation techniques, prepare growing materials, and maintain appropriate growing conditions.

03 — Growth Documentation

Record observations throughout cultivation to evaluate growth progression and identify challenges.

04 — Troubleshooting

Investigate contamination and growth challenges through adjustments to environmental conditions and cultivation procedures.

04 / Engineering Applications

This research provides opportunities to apply principles of experimental design, environmental monitoring, biological systems analysis, and process optimization.

These concepts connect to broader engineering fields such as biotechnology, bioprocess engineering, and controlled biological production.

05 / Skills & Learning Outcomes

Through this ongoing project, I continue developing my ability to monitor biological systems, document observations, troubleshoot experimental challenges, and evaluate environmental variables.

06 / Future Development

Future opportunities include developing more systematic environmental monitoring, comparing cultivation conditions, and analyzing recorded observations to better understand growth trends.
